The complete moving house checklist

An eight-week countdown to moving day — what to do, when, so nothing gets forgotten.

A good move is mostly planning. Use this countdown from the day you agree a sale or sign a tenancy, and moving day will be far calmer.

Eight weeks to go

  • Get removal quotes and book your survey. Popular dates (Fridays, month ends, summer) go early.
  • Start decluttering room by room — loft, garage and shed first.
  • If you rent, check your notice period and what the landlord expects at check-out.
  • Start a moving folder for quotes, contracts and contact numbers.

Six weeks to go

  • Book your removal company and any packing, storage or cleaning.
  • Order boxes and packing materials (we deliver them free with a booked move).
  • Arrange time off work, and childcare or pet care for moving day.
  • Measure large furniture and check it will fit in the new home.

Four weeks to go

  • Start packing things you won’t need: books, spare bedding, out-of-season clothes, ornaments.
  • Tell your council, utilities, broadband provider, bank, employer, GP, dentist, insurers and DVLA.
  • Set up Royal Mail redirection.
  • Arrange parking at both properties if access is tight.

Two weeks to go

  • Confirm your date and times with us, and give us any access details for the new property.
  • Run down the freezer and food cupboards.
  • Dispose of anything we can’t carry: paint, gas bottles, chemicals and fuel.

The day before

  • Defrost the fridge-freezer.
  • Pack your first-night box and a bag of essentials.
  • Charge your phone and keep keys, documents and valuables together.

Moving day

  • Take meter readings and photographs.
  • Do a final walk round: lofts, cupboards, sheds and behind doors.
  • Leave keys and manuals for the new owners.
  • At the new home, show the crew where each room is and put the kettle on.
