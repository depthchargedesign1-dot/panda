Removal tips & FAQ

Answers to the questions we’re asked most, and our crews’ best advice for a smoother move.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What area do you cover?

We’re based in Helmsley and cover everywhere within 30 miles — including York, Malton, Pickering, Kirkbymoorside, Thirsk, Easingwold, Northallerton, Stokesley, Ripon, Scarborough and Whitby. We also do long-distance moves to anywhere in the UK and arrange European moves.

How much does a removal cost?

Every move is different, so we give a free, fixed-price quote after a quick survey (in person or by video). The price depends on the volume of belongings, access at both ends, the distance and any extras like packing or storage.

Are my belongings insured?

Yes. Our moves include goods-in-transit insurance and we hold public liability insurance. Ask us for details of the cover and any options to increase it for high-value items.

How far in advance should I book?

Four to six weeks is ideal, particularly for Fridays, month ends and the summer. We can often help at shorter notice — just call.

Do I need to empty drawers and wardrobes?

Light clothing can usually stay in chests of drawers, and we bring wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes. Heavy items, liquids and anything fragile should be packed separately.

What won’t you move?

We can’t carry hazardous items such as gas bottles, petrol, paint thinners, fireworks or ammunition. We’ll also ask you to keep valuables, jewellery, important documents and medication with you.

Do you work weekends?

Yes, we move on Saturdays as standard and on Sundays by arrangement — handy for business moves.

Can you store my things between houses?

Yes. We offer short and long-term storage, collected from your old home and delivered to your new one when you’re ready.

Do you take a deposit?

A small deposit secures your date. The balance is due before or on the day of the move — your quote will set out the terms clearly.

What if it rains?

This is Yorkshire — we’re ready for it. We use floor protection, covers and blankets so your belongings stay dry and your floors stay clean.

From our crews

Top removal tips

1. Start with what you don’t use

Declutter before you pack. Every item you sell, donate or recycle is one less thing to wrap, move and unpack — and it can bring your quote down.

2. Use the right boxes

Small boxes for books and heavy items, larger boxes for light things like bedding. Keep each box under about 20kg so it can be lifted safely.

3. Label every box on the side

Write the room and a short description on two sides (not just the top — boxes get stacked). Mark fragile boxes clearly.

4. Pack plates on their edge

Plates travel far better stood on edge like records, each wrapped, with crumpled paper at the bottom and top of the box.

5. Pack a first-night box

Kettle, mugs, tea, phone chargers, toilet roll, bedding, towels, basic tools, medication and something for the kids and pets. Keep it with you.

6. Photograph your cables

Snap the back of the TV and router before unplugging so setting up is easy, and bag screws and remotes together.

7. Defrost the freezer

Switch off and defrost the fridge-freezer at least 24 hours before moving day, and leave the doors ajar to stop it smelling.

8. Tell people you’re moving

Council tax, utilities, broadband, DVLA, your bank, GP and dentist, and set up Royal Mail redirection for a few months.
