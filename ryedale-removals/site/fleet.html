Choosing the right size

We’ll recommend the right vehicle during your survey. Where a lorry can’t reach your door, we use a smaller van to shuttle, so nothing is carried further than it needs to be.

Small van

Single items, small flats, student moves, auction collections

Load space
Around 10 m³
Crew
1–2 people
Typical move
Studio / single items

Best for: Narrow lanes, city centres and quick local jobs.

Luton van

Covered box van with tail-lift for one and two-bedroom homes

Load space
Around 20 m³
Crew
2 people
Typical move
1–2 bedroom home

Best for: Cottages, flats and part-loads — with a tail-lift for heavy items.

Removal lorry

Large purpose-built removal vehicle for family homes and offices

Around 40 m³+
Crew
3–4 people

3–5 bedroom home

Best for: Family homes, farmhouses and office relocations in one trip.
