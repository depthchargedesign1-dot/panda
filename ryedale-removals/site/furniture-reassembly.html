Furniture dismantling and reassembly

Beds, wardrobes, tables and flat-pack taken apart, moved safely and rebuilt in your new home — fixings bagged and labelled.

Get a free quote 01632 960 123

Big beds, fitted wardrobes, extending tables and flat-pack furniture rarely fit through cottage doors in one piece. Our crews bring the tools, take items apart carefully and rebuild them in your new home.

Nothing lost

Every screw, bolt and fitting is bagged and labelled with the item it belongs to, so your furniture goes back together exactly as it came apart.

Rebuilt where you want it

Tell us which room each piece goes in and we’ll rebuild it there — so your beds are made up and ready on the first night.

Flat-pack assembly

Bought new furniture for the new house? We can assemble flat-pack items for you, too.

What’s included

  • Beds and bunk beds
  • Wardrobes and sliding-door robes
  • Dining and extending tables
  • Flat-pack furniture assembly
  • Wall-mounted TVs taken down
  • Fixings bagged and labelled

Where we work

We’re based in Helmsley and provide dismantling & reassembly everywhere within 30 miles, including Helmsley, Kirkbymoorside, Easingwold, Pickering, Thirsk, Malton, Stokesley, Northallerton, Ripon, York, Whitby and Scarborough, plus all the villages in between. See our full coverage area.

Questions about dismantling & reassembly

Is dismantling included in a house move?

Standard beds and wardrobes are included in our house removal quotes. Large fitted or complex items are noted during the survey.
