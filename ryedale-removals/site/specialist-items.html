Piano, antique and specialist furniture removals

Pianos, antiques, artwork, grandfather clocks, gym equipment and heavy furniture moved with the right kit and know-how.

Get a free quote 01632 960 123

Some items need more than strong arms. Upright pianos, family antiques, long-case clocks, artwork, range cookers, safes and gym equipment all need the right equipment, planning and a crew who has done it before.

Pianos

Upright pianos are moved on padded piano skids and wheeled boards, fully wrapped and strapped. For grand pianos and very difficult access we’ll survey first and quote accordingly.

Antiques and artwork

Period furniture, mirrors and paintings are wrapped in acid-free tissue, bubble wrap and blankets, with custom cartons for artwork. Ideal for antique dealers and auction houses too.

Heavy and awkward items

Stone garden ornaments, cast-iron baths, range cookers, safes, hot tubs and gym equipment — tell us what it is and we’ll bring the right kit and enough people.

What’s included

  • Upright piano moves
  • Antique and period furniture
  • Artwork and mirror cartons
  • Long-case and grandfather clocks

  • Dealer and auction deliveries
