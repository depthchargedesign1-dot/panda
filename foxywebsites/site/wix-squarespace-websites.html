A proper design on an easy platform

Wix and Squarespace make it easy to keep your own website up to date. What they don't do is decide what your site should say, how it should look or how people will find it. That's where most DIY sites fall down.

Our design team plans the pages, designs them to match your brand, and helps you write clear text that tells customers what you do and how to get in touch. We set up bookings, forms or a small shop if you need one, and connect your Google Business Profile so you show up in local searches.

It's ideal for trades, salons, cafés, makers and small service businesses that want to look professional without a big budget.

Good fit if you...

  • are a trade or local service business
  • want to update the site yourself
  • need bookings or enquiry forms
  • sell a small range of products
  • want to be found on Google Maps

Questions

Questions people ask

Wix or Squarespace: which is better?

Squarespace has polished templates and suits image-led businesses. Wix is more flexible for bookings and add-ons. We'll suggest the better fit for what you need.

Can you redesign my existing Wix site?

Yes. We can refresh the design, rewrite pages and fix SEO problems on your current site.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up the basics properly: page titles, descriptions, Search Console and your Google Business Profile. Rankings take time, but your site will be set up to be found.

Who pays for the Wix or Squarespace plan?

You do, directly, so the site stays in your name. We help you choose the right plan.
