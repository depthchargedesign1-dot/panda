Built for exactly what you need

Hand-built websites load quickly, have no monthly platform fees and do exactly what you need, nothing more. They're ideal for landing pages, campaign sites and small business sites that rarely change.

We also build custom tools that plug into your existing shop. Our own Foxy Printing shop uses a product designer we built, where customers add names, fonts and logos to a hoodie, see it on real photos and order, and print-ready files are made automatically.

If your shop needs something no app does well, such as a price calculator, a custom checkout step or a link to your supplier's system, we can build it.

Questions

Questions people ask

Can I edit a hand-built site?

For sites that change often we'll recommend a platform instead. For sites that rarely change, we make updates for you quickly on a care plan.

Can you add a custom tool to my Shopify shop?

Yes. We build designers, calculators and integrations that work inside Shopify and other platforms.

Do you host the site?

We can set up hosting in your name or use your existing host. Hand-built sites are cheap to host.

How much does a landing page cost?

It depends on the content and features. We give a fixed price once we know what you need.
