Rescue & repair

Broken site? Google Shopping switched off? We'll fix it.

Send us the error message, the screenshot, or just "it's stopped working". We find the cause, fix it, and tell you in plain English what went wrong.

Website repairsGoogle Shopping fixes

Google Merchant Center · Diagnostics 6 issues · 312 products affected
  • SuspendedMisrepresentation

    Account suspended. No products showing on Google.

    Fixed: contact details, returns and delivery pages rewritten, checkout made clear, review requested.

  • DisapprovedMissing value [gtin]

    Products need barcodes or a brand exemption.

    Fixed: GTINs, MPNs and brand added, or identifier_exists set correctly for own-made items.

  • DisapprovedMismatched value (page crawl) [price]

    The feed price doesn't match the product page.

    Fixed: feed sync and sale prices corrected, structured data repaired.

  • LimitedImage too small / promotional overlay

    Images are blurry or have text on top.

    Fixed: clean main images replaced at full size.

  • DisapprovedMissing shipping information

    No delivery cost set for the UK.

    Fixed: delivery services and costs set up to match the checkout.

  • DisapprovedUnavailable desktop landing page

    Links in the feed go to a 404 page.

    Fixed: broken URLs and redirects repaired, feed re-fetched.

Questions

Things people ask us.

Can you work on a site someone else built?

Yes. Most of our fix and listing work is on existing shops. We'll take a look first, tell you what we find, and only then quote.

Can you move my eBay shop to my own website?

Yes. We bring your products, photos and descriptions across to Shopify, BigCommerce or WooCommerce, and keep your eBay shop running alongside it if you want both.

Will one stock list work across my site, eBay, Amazon and Etsy?

Yes. We set up connector apps so an item sold on one channel comes off stock everywhere, and the orders land in one place.

Do you use AI? Will my site look generic?

Your site is designed by people, not generated. We use the latest AI models for the legwork, like first drafts of product descriptions, building bulk listing files and checking feeds for errors. The design, the decisions and the final check are all down to our design team.

Where are you based?

Our team is based in Helmsley, North Yorkshire, and works with businesses across the UK. Most projects are run by phone, email and video call.

How much does it cost?

Every job is different, so we give you a fixed price after a free look at what you need. No hourly surprises.
