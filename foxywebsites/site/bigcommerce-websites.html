When BigCommerce is the right choice

BigCommerce suits businesses with thousands of products, complicated options or trade customers who need their own prices. It includes features that other platforms charge apps for, such as customer groups, price lists and built-in marketplace channels.

Our design team builds BigCommerce stores on the Stencil theme framework, so the design is fully yours and still easy to update. We import your catalogue with clean categories, options and images, then connect the channels you sell on.

We also look after existing BigCommerce stores: fixing theme problems, speeding up slow pages, cleaning up product data and sorting Google Shopping feeds.

Good fit if you...

  • have thousands of products or variants
  • sell to trade and retail customers
  • need multi-channel selling built in
  • want fewer monthly app costs
  • already run BigCommerce and need help

Questions

Questions people ask

Is BigCommerce better than Shopify?

It depends on your range and customers. BigCommerce is often better for large catalogues and trade pricing; Shopify is often simpler for smaller shops. We'll recommend the one that suits you.

Can you import products from a spreadsheet?

Yes. We clean and map your spreadsheet or supplier feed into BigCommerce's import format, including options, images and categories.

Can trade customers see different prices?

Yes. We set up customer groups and price lists so approved trade accounts see their own pricing.

Do you work on existing BigCommerce stores?

Yes. Send us the store link and what's wrong, and we'll take a look before quoting.
