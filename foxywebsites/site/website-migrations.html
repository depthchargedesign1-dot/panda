Website migration, planned properly

Moving your shop to a new platform can cut costs and make it easier to run, but a rushed move can lose products, break links and drop your Google rankings overnight.

Our design team plans the move first: what comes across, what gets tidied up on the way and how old URLs map to new ones. We move products, images, variants, customers and order history, and build the new design on a test site so you can check it before anything goes live.

On launch day we switch the domain, set up 301 redirects for every old page, and check orders, emails, payments and Google Search Console afterwards.

Popular moves

  • eBay shop to your own Shopify site
  • WooCommerce to Shopify
  • Magento to Shopify or BigCommerce
  • Wix or Squarespace to Shopify
  • BigCommerce to Shopify, or the reverse

Questions

Questions people ask

Can I move my eBay shop to my own website?

Yes. We bring your listings, photos and descriptions across to Shopify or BigCommerce, and you can keep selling on eBay alongside it.

Will my Google rankings drop?

A properly redirected move keeps most rankings. We map every old URL and monitor Search Console after launch.

Will my shop be offline during the move?

No. We build the new shop on a test site and switch over when it's ready, usually with no downtime.

Can you move customer passwords?

Platforms don't allow passwords to be moved for security reasons, so customers set a new one at their next login.
