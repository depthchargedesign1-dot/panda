Social shops connected to your website

More and more shopping starts on social media. A product spotted in a TikTok video or an Instagram post can be bought in a couple of taps, if your shop is connected.

Our design team sets up your TikTok Shop and Meta commerce account, connects your product catalogue from Shopify or another platform, and makes sure products are approved and tagged ready for posts and videos.

We also set up other places to sell, such as OnBuy, and your Google Business Profile with products, so local customers find you on Google Maps.

Good fit if you...

  • post products on social media
  • want customers to buy inside the app
  • already sell on Shopify
  • want more UK marketplaces
  • need everything managed from one place

Questions

Questions people ask

Can you set up TikTok Shop in the UK?

Yes. We help you register, connect your catalogue and set up shipping and returns.

Do I need Shopify for social shops?

No, but it makes syncing much easier. We can also connect BigCommerce and WooCommerce catalogues.

Can products be tagged in Instagram posts?

Yes, once your shop is approved, products can be tagged in posts, reels and stories.

What is OnBuy?

OnBuy is a UK online marketplace, similar to Amazon and eBay. It can be a useful extra channel for some products.
