Product listing and SEO product descriptions

A shop is only as good as its product pages. Thin descriptions, missing sizes and blurry photos lose sales and rankings. Doing it properly for hundreds or thousands of products takes time most business owners don't have.

Our design team prepares your products in bulk: titles, descriptions, variants, SKUs, prices, stock and images, formatted for your website and each marketplace. Descriptions are written in friendly UK English around the words customers search for, with sizes, materials and delivery facts included.

AI tools help us with first drafts and data checks at scale, and every description is checked and edited by a person before it goes live. That's how we keep quality high on big catalogues.

Good fit if you...

  • have a spreadsheet of products to load
  • have thin or copied descriptions
  • sell the same range on several channels
  • need marketplace-ready images
  • want personalised product options

Questions

Questions people ask

How many products can you list?

From a handful to tens of thousands. We work in batches and check each one before it goes live.

Will the descriptions be unique?

Yes. Each product gets its own description. We never paste the same text across a range.

Do you use AI to write descriptions?

AI helps with first drafts and checking data at scale. Our design team edits and approves every description so it's accurate and sounds like your business.

Can you create product images?

Yes. We place your artwork onto real product photos and create clean white-background images for marketplaces.
