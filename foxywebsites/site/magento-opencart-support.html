Support for shops that still need to trade

Many businesses still run on Magento 1, Magento 2 or OpenCart. They often work, but updates are hard, developers are expensive and extensions break when anything changes. Older versions no longer get security updates at all.

Our design team can stabilise your shop first: fixing errors, broken checkouts and extension conflicts, and cleaning up after hacks. Then we can plan a move to Shopify or BigCommerce, carrying over your products, customers and order history.

The most important part of a move is keeping your Google rankings. We map every old URL to its new page with 301 redirects, so customers and Google land in the right place.

Signs it's time to move

  • your version no longer gets security updates
  • small changes cost a lot of developer time
  • extensions break after updates
  • the site is slow on phones
  • hosting bills keep going up

Questions

Questions people ask

Can you keep my Magento shop running for now?

Yes. We can fix urgent problems and keep it stable while you decide what to do next.

Will I lose my Google rankings if I move?

Not if it's done properly. We map every old page to a new one with 301 redirects and check Search Console after launch.

Can customer accounts move too?

Customer details and order history can move. For security, customers usually set a new password the first time they log in.

How long does a migration take?

Most moves take four to eight weeks, depending on the size of the catalogue and any custom features.
