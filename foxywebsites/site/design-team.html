Why experience matters

Anyone can put a theme on a website. Knowing what to change, and why, is what takes years. Our design team has worked through every stage of the web: hand-coded pages, the first online shops, WordPress, Shopify, Amazon, Etsy and today's world of shops that sell on five channels at once.

That experience shows up in the details customers notice: product pages that answer their questions, delivery information they can trust, and a checkout that just works. It also shows up in the details Google and the marketplaces check, from structured data and redirects to item specifics and Merchant Center policies.

Because we're also a design and print business, we think about your brand as a whole. Your website, your eBay shop, your packaging and your printed materials should all look like the same company.

30

Years on the web

Every version of the internet, worked through.

The platforms change every few years. The basics of a shop that sells don't: trust, clear information, good photos and being easy to find.

  1. Mid 90sHand-coded HTML

    Table layouts, dial-up, guestbooks. Learning how the web really works, from the code up.

  2. Early 2000sThe first online shops

    Shopping carts, payment gateways and the first eBay shops.

  3. Late 2000sWordPress and social

    Sites the owner can edit themselves. Blogs, SEO and Facebook pages arrive.

  4. 2010sShopify, Amazon and Etsy

    Hosted shop platforms, marketplace sellers, mobile-first design and Google Shopping feeds.

  5. 2020sEverywhere at once

    One stock list feeding a website, eBay, Amazon, Etsy, TikTok Shop and Google.

  6. NowDesign-led, AI-assisted

    The same hands-on team, with AI doing the repetitive jobs.

About working with us

Who will I deal with?

The same small design team from first chat to launch and after. You won't be passed between departments.

How do you use AI?

As a tool for repetitive work: first drafts of product descriptions, building bulk listing files, and scanning feeds and code for errors. The design, decisions and final checks are done by people.
