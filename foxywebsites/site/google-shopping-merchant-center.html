Google Merchant Center, set up and fixed

Google Shopping can send steady, ready-to-buy traffic to your shop, including free product listings that cost nothing per click. But Merchant Center is strict. Missing barcodes, price mismatches, unclear returns pages or contact details can get products disapproved, or the whole account suspended.

Our design team sets up Merchant Center, verifies your website, and connects a clean product feed from Shopify, BigCommerce or WooCommerce. We set the right identifiers, categories, delivery and returns settings so products are approved.

If your account has been suspended for misrepresentation, we review your site the way Google does: business details, policies, checkout, pricing and product claims. We fix the causes on your site first, then request the review.

Common problems we fix

  • Account suspended for misrepresentation
  • Missing value [gtin] or brand
  • Price or availability mismatch
  • Images too small or with overlays
  • Missing delivery or returns settings

Questions

Questions people ask

Why was my Merchant Center account suspended?

The most common reason is misrepresentation: Google couldn't confirm who you are or found unclear policies, pricing or claims. We review the site, fix the issues and request a review.

What does "Missing value [gtin]" mean?

Google wants a barcode for branded products. We add the right GTINs, or mark your own-made products correctly so they don't need one.

Are Google Shopping listings free?

Yes, free listings are available alongside paid Shopping ads. We set up both, starting with free listings.

How long does a suspension review take?

Google usually replies within a few days to a couple of weeks. The important thing is to fix the causes before requesting it.
