Shopify web design, done properly

Shopify is the platform we build on most, because it lets small teams run a serious online shop without worrying about servers or security patches. The catch is that a default theme and a few apps only get you so far. Shops that sell well have product pages that answer every question, clear delivery information and collections that make browsing easy.

Our design team plans the shop around how your customers buy. We set up the structure, design the pages, load and tidy your products, and connect the sales channels. If you sell personalised products, we can build a live preview so customers see their name, photo or logo on the product before they order.

We run our own Shopify shop, Foxy Printing, with a custom product designer and thousands of products, so we know where shops tend to go wrong and how to fix it.

Good fit if you...

  • are starting a new online shop
  • have outgrown a free theme
  • sell on eBay or Etsy and want your own site
  • need personalised or made-to-order products
  • want Google Shopping set up from day one

Questions

Questions people ask

How long does a Shopify store take?

A straightforward shop with up to a few hundred products usually takes two to four weeks, depending on how quickly we get your content and decisions. Bigger catalogues and custom features take longer, and we agree the timeline in your quote.

Do I need to pay for Shopify separately?

Yes. The Shopify subscription, any paid apps and your theme are paid to Shopify directly, so the shop stays in your name. We help you pick the right plan.

Can you move my existing shop to Shopify?

Yes. We move products, customers and order history from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix or eBay, and set up redirects so you keep your Google rankings.

Can customers personalise products?

Yes. We can add personalisation fields or build a full product designer with a live preview, like the leavers hoodie designer on our own shop.
