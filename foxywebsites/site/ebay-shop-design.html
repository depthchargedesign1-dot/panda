eBay shop design and listing, start to finish

eBay still brings in a huge number of buyers, but it's strict about how listings are built. Templates with active content, missing item specifics or contact details in descriptions can get listings blocked or buried in search.

Our design team sets up your eBay Store properly: business policies, store categories, a branded logo and billboard, and a listing template designed to eBay's current rules that looks good on a phone. Then we write and load your products in bulk using eBay's upload templates, with item specifics filled in so buyers can find them.

If you also sell on your own website, we connect the two so stock, prices and orders stay in step. We've built and loaded large eBay ranges for our own business, so we know the pitfalls.

Good fit if you...

  • are opening an eBay Store
  • want your eBay shop to match your brand
  • have hundreds or thousands of items to list
  • keep overselling between eBay and your site
  • have listings blocked or hidden

Questions

Questions people ask

Can you design my eBay shop?

Yes. We create your store logo, billboard banner, description and categories, and a listing template that matches your brand.

Can you list thousands of products?

Yes. We prepare your products in eBay's bulk upload format, with item specifics and photos, and load them in batches.

Can eBay and my website share stock?

Yes. We set up a connector app so an item sold on one channel comes off stock on the other.

Why are my eBay listings being blocked?

Common causes are links or contact details in the description, active content in templates, missing item specifics or brand rights issues. We find the cause and fix it.
