Website fixes on any platform

When your website or shop breaks, every hour costs you orders and trust. It might be a theme update that broke the layout, a plugin conflict, a payment gateway that stopped working, an expired SSL certificate or a hack.

Our design team has been fixing websites for 30 years, on everything from hand-coded HTML to Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, Wix and Squarespace. We work out what changed, fix the cause and check everything else still works.

Once it's fixed, we explain what happened and how to stop it happening again, whether that's updates, backups, better hosting or a care plan.

Questions

Questions people ask

How quickly can you fix my website?

Urgent problems like a site being down or a broken checkout get looked at first. Call us on 01439 771468 and we'll tell you what we can do.

Can you fix a site someone else built?

Yes. Most of our repair work is on sites built by other people.

My site has been hacked. What should I do?

Change your hosting and admin passwords, then contact us. We'll clean the site, close the hole that let the hacker in and help you get removed from any Google warnings.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the problem. We take a look first, tell you what we've found and give you a fixed price before we do the work.
