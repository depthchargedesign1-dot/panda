# Etsy UK: personalised pint glasses (research, 9 Oct 2026)

Owner asked "check etsy for personalsied pint glasses". Etsy pages could not be fetched from here, so this is from search-result snippets of Etsy UK market pages (undated, prices/review counts vary between captures). Treat rankings as estimates.

## What sells
- **Name pint with "Est./Since [year]"**: the dominant style; top listing (CherishUs, engraved 568ml name pint) has a Bestseller badge and ~5–8k reviews. https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/pint_glass_personalised
- **Dad / Father's Day** ("Dad's Pint", "Daddy's Pint", name + initial). https://www.etsy.com/market/dad_pint_glasses
- **Face / photo pints** (custom face cut-out + name, 20oz): own Etsy UK market page; lead listing ~7.3k reviews at ~£13.80, a "Best Dad" kids-photo pint ~16.2k reviews. Pet-face pints a sub-niche. Biggest *printed* niche. https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/personalised_face_pint_glass , https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/custom_pint_glasses_with_photo
- **Wedding party** (best man, groomsman, usher), ~£4 vinyl tuxedo to ~£28. https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/personalised_best_man_pint_glass
- Generic football design with name/message; pub/tavern "[Name]'s Tavern".

## Print methods
Mostly laser engraved/etched ("won't peel"); vinyl at the cheap end; UV printing rare; **no UK seller found marketing UV DTF full-colour pints** — our gap: full colour, photo-quality.

## Prices / options
- Single pint ~£5–£18, typical £10–£17; engraved bestsellers £15–£22.50; face pints ~£13–£19; free UK delivery common, gift box often included.
- Options: name (often 12 chars), year, 1–2 message lines, photo upload (background removed), sometimes text colour.
- Titles stack keywords: "Personalised Pint Glass | Gift for Him | Birthday Christmas Fathers Day Dad 568ml".
