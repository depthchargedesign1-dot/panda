# Personalised photo party face masks: the big push (owner priority, 2 Oct 2026)

**Goal:** become the go-to UK shop for personalised photo face masks (your own face on a card mask), especially for **stag dos and hen parties**, with **no minimum (order 1)** and great multipack prices. The range gets its own collection, its own category in the menu, and a full Google Merchant Center and Google Ads setup.

## 1. Now vs. the plan
**Now:** 31 near-identical listings ("5 X …" to "50 X Personalised Custom Photo Party Face Masks"), each with DIY and Pre-cut options, plus two single-mask listings (£2.49 and £2.79). The copy is in ALL CAPS, there are no SEO fields, and the photos are old.

That's bad for Google: 31 thin duplicates competing with each other. It's bad for shoppers too, who have to hunt for their pack size.

**Plan:** one strong hero listing plus occasion landing listings, each with **Style × Quantity** options. Retire the 31 old listings with 301 redirects to the hero, so no Google history is lost.

## 4. Google Merchant Center (feed through the Google & YouTube app)
- **Titles:** keyword first, ≤150 characters, e.g. "Personalised Stag Do Face Masks – Groom's Photo – Card Party Masks, Order 1+".
- **Fields:**
  - custom_product = true (no GTIN);
  - condition new; age_group adult; gender unisex;
  - category Costumes & Accessories > Masks;
  - **custom_label_0 = personalised-masks**, custom_label_1 = occasion (stag / hen / birthday …), custom_label_2 = price band.
- **Main images:** no text or watermarks; at least 800 × 800.
- **Shipping:** set real dispatch and delivery times in Merchant Center (ASK), so Google can show "delivery by" dates. Stag and hen buyers search close to the date.
- **Trademarks:** keep all celebrity and brand names out of these listings. They're the customer's own photos, so these are the safest masks to advertise.

## 5. Google Ads (starter setup)
**Campaigns:**
1. **Performance Max – Personalised Masks:** feed filtered to custom_label_0 = personalised-masks. Start at **£15–£25 a day**, with a target ROAS once there are 30+ conversions.
2. **Search – Stag & Hen**, two ad groups:
   - Stag: stag do face masks, groom face masks, stag do masks of the groom, personalised stag masks.
   - Hen: hen party face masks, bride face masks, hen do face masks, personalised hen masks.
3. **Search – Personalised Masks (generic):** personalised face masks, custom face masks, photo face masks, face masks with your face, cardboard face masks of a person, make your own face mask.
4. **Search – Birthday & Events** (lower budget): birthday face masks, 40th / 50th birthday face masks, retirement face masks, wedding photo booth masks.
