### What's working
- **Personalisation at scale.** You already run thousands of name/photo SKUs. The new ranges plug into the same workflow.
- **Niche fandoms with little competition:** retro gaming cases, magnets and keyrings, darts, and PerfectDraft skins.
- **Price points that convert:** cards at £2.48 and mugs at £6.99.

### What needs fixing before the relaunch
1. **Stale and empty collections.** At least 30 collections have 0 products (e.g. *3DO Cases, Art Posters, Cheeky Mugs, I Like… Mugs, NES Canvas, Xbox 360 Cases*). Duplicates also exist: *Football Team Mugs ×2, Father's Day Gifts ×2, PS1 Magnets ×2, Personalised Mouse Mats ×2, Santa Sacks ×2, Emmerdale ×2*. Archive or merge them so the new menu and site search stay clean.
2. **Out-of-date events** (Coronation 2023, Euros 2024, VE Day 80 in 2025, social-distancing stickers, face coverings) still sit in the catalogue. Unpublish them, or move them into an "Archive" collection that isn't in the menu.
3. **£0.00 products.** Many replacement game cases show a £0.00 price (they rely on a hidden options app). With the new theme you can use real variants ("Case only / Cover only / Case + cover") so prices show correctly in Google Shopping and on product cards.
4. **Blank product types and heavy tag rules.** Most products have no Product Type, so collections run on 100+ tag rules, and the "ALL MUGS" collection works by matching price = £6.99. Set Product Type on the big families so storefront filters work.
5. **Negative inventory** on many cards and cases (e.g. −128). These are made-to-order, so set them to *untracked* inventory.
6. **Brand and IP risk (important).** The current range leans on other people's intellectual property: "THEME INSPIRED" character cards (Peppa Pig, Harry Potter, TMNT, Disney), celebrity face masks, reproduction "signed" prints, and console-branded game covers and posters. Rights holders send takedown notices to Shopify, Etsy and Amazon, and Shopify can restrict payouts or close stores over repeated IP complaints. The new ranges in this plan are **original, personalised designs** on purpose. They are a safer base to grow the brand on. Phase the licensed-style lines down over time, or get licences for the ones that sell best.

## 2. What the new machines unlock

| Machine | Best at | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Roland VersaUV LEF-300** (UV flatbed, 762 × 330 mm bed, 100 mm height, CMYK + white + gloss) | Acrylic, slate, wood, metal, phone cases, golf balls (with jig), dart flights, pens and promo items, spot-gloss "raised" business cards | Flat or near-flat items only (no rotary). Use jigs to batch-print 8–24 items per run. Large signs need outsourcing. _If you meant a VersaCAMM print-and-cut, the sticker, label and vinyl lines in this plan still apply._ |
| **UV DTF** | Anything hard and curved: glass can cups, tumblers, bottles, baubles, candles, jars, helmets, controllers | Not food-contact on drinking rims; keep prints 10 mm below the lip. Sell spare transfers wholesale too. |
| **DTF clothing** | Tees, hoodies, baby grows, bibs, bags, aprons, pet bandanas, caps, workwear | Use good blanks (Gildan, AWDis, Larkwood) and press consistently. Sell gang sheets by the metre to fill downtime. |
| **Flatbed cutter** | Boxes (Christmas Eve, cupcake, golf ball, favour, mailer), game cases, die-cut stickers, pop-up cards, toppers, jigsaws | Make a die-line library once and reuse it for every theme. |

**The big idea:** sell gift **bundles** that use two or three machines at once, e.g. a printed golf ball set in a printed box, a mug in a personalised mug box, or a Christmas Eve box with a name bauble and matching PJs. Bundles raise order value and are hard for single-process competitors to copy.

## 4. The new theme: "Foxy Pop"

Lives in [`../theme`](../theme). It's a Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme built for this catalogue:

- **Vistaprint-style layout, much more colourful.** Five brand colours (foxy orange, hot pink, party purple, teal, sunshine yellow) rotate across departments, tiles and badges. All colours and fonts are editable in the theme editor.
- **Multi-department mega menu.** 11 departments, unlimited columns, 3 levels, an optional promo tile per department, and a mobile drawer with accordions. It's driven by the **Claude Mega Menu** (`foxy-mega-menu`), already created in your store, so you manage the hundreds of categories in *Online Store → Navigation* with no code.
- **Live personaliser on product pages.** Customers type a name or message, or upload a photo, and see it drawn instantly on a mockup:
  - **Card:** front and inside views (name and age on the front, message inside in a handwriting font)
  - **Drinkware:** mug, can cup, tumbler, glass or bottle, with the design wrapped round the cylinder
  - **Apparel:** tee, baby grow, tote or bandana, in the variant colour
  - **Bauble / Santa key**, **box** (Christmas Eve box, game case, cupcake box), **flat** (acrylic, slate, wood, metal, golf ball, LED light)
  - Font and colour pickers, a character counter, and a "checked my spelling" tick before add to basket
  - Values are saved as order line-item properties, photo uploads included, so they appear on the order in your admin
  - For your existing cards, set a `foxy.preview_zone` metafield to print the name straight onto the real card artwork
- Also included: hero banners, category circles, a Christmas countdown, product grids, a "how it works" section, new-machine feature tiles, reviews, a business-printing strip, newsletter, collection filters and sort, cart showing personalisation details, and customer account pages.
- Lints clean with Shopify Theme Check. The only warnings are for the Google Fonts the personaliser uses.

## 5. Go-live checklist

1. **Upload the theme:** Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip → `dist/foxy-pop-theme.zip`. It installs **unpublished**, so your live "NEW WAREHOUSE 2020" theme is untouched.
2. Customise → Header: confirm the menu is **Claude Mega Menu**, upload your logo, and add promo images to the department blocks.
3. Homepage: pick collections for the Bestsellers and Christmas rows and the category circles, and replace the placeholder reviews with real ones.
4. Products: add photos to the drafts (or let the live preview stand in as the main image at first), check prices and costs, then set them **Active** and publish the `new-…` collections, starting with Wave 1.
5. Cards: assign the **product.card** template to card products (bulk edit → Theme template) to switch on the front-and-inside personaliser.
6. Tidy the catalogue (section 1): archive empty, duplicate and expired collections, and fix £0.00 prices.
7. Preview everything, then **Publish** the theme.
