**ASK the owner. These answers decide what we can promise:**
1. **VG2 ink set.** Is it CMYK plus Lc/Lm/Lk, plus White, or plus Orange?
   - If white is fitted, we can print white under colour on clear and metallic vinyl. That gives true clear stickers, coloured metallic, and white-ink clings.
   - Without white, clear stickers have no white, and metallic stickers show the metal through the ink. That still looks good for gold and silver logos.
2. **UCJV150-160 ink.** Is it 4-colour only, or has white been added?
3. **Media stocked or wanted, and brands** (we name brands only in the fact sheet, never in claims we can't back up):
   - monomeric vinyl (short-term);
   - polymeric or cast vinyl (vehicles);
   - chrome, gold or brushed metallic;
   - clear;
   - static cling, clear and white;
   - one-way vision;
   - polyester (PET) or synthetic tearproof paper;
   - floor graphic laminate (anti-slip rated);
   - magnetic sheet.
4. **Laminator.** Do we have one, and what width? Gloss, matt and anti-slip?
5. **Lifetimes we can honestly quote.** For example, "outdoor life up to X years when laminated" depends on the vinyl brand's data sheet. We'll quote the data sheet and nothing else.
6. **Lead times** for sticker orders and for full vehicle or caravan kits.

  - **UV DTF / DTF Gang Sheets by the Metre**, on the Inkfuse machines.

## 5. Motorbike, caravan and motorhome replacement vinyl: rules

Replacement decals are a strong search niche. UK sellers charge about £20–£95 for caravan and motorhome swirl kits and about £7–£65 for motorbike kits. But most of what's sold reproduces the makers' **logos and trademarks** (caravan makers like Swift, Bailey, Elddis and Auto-Trail; motorbike makers like Honda, Yamaha and Ducati). Under CLAUDE.md:
- **Sell:** original swirl, stripe and wave designs; custom name and model plates; colour-matched stripes; race numbers; customers' own designs.
- **Describe fitment only:** e.g. "replacement-style side swirl to fit a 2005–2010 two-berth caravan, about 3.2 m". **Never** print a maker's logo or name badge unless the owner has a licence. Add the Brands disclaimer and the `third-party-name` tag to any listing that mentions a maker or model.
- **Measure-to-fit:** variants for left, right or pair. The customer enters the length of the old graphic, or uploads photos of the old decals with a tape measure in shot.
- Offer **colour matching** to the old faded graphic (customer photo or swatch, no guaranteed match). Offer **fitting instructions** (wet or dry application) as a PDF.
