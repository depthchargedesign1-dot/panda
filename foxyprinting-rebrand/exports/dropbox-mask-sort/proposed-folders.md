## C. Possible later split of the TV folder (not used in this plan)

`2026 TV SHOWS AND STARS` would hold about 2,700 images after this sort. If the owner wants it easier to browse, a later pass could split it into `2026 TV PRESENTERS`, `2026 REALITY TV` (Love Island, TOWIE, Geordie Shore, Made in Chelsea, Real Housewives already have subfolders) and `2026 TV AND SOAP ACTORS`. That would touch thousands of files already in place, so it is left out of this plan; the category for each file is in `plan.csv` (`category` column: `tv` or `reality`) if it is wanted later.
