Little Britain character face masks - print and cut files (Foxy Printing)
=========================================================================
Each mask has:
  <Name>.pdf  - SRA4 sheet (225 x 320 mm), face fitted in the A4 area (210 x 297 mm).
                Layers: "Artwork" (the face) and "CUT" (magenta 0.1 mm cut line, 2 mm inside the edge, plus eye holes).
  <Name>.svg  - same layout, opens in Illustrator (groups "Artwork" and "CUT").
  <Name> - check.png - preview: check the eye holes sit on the pupils before cutting.

Steps (Intec ColorCut):
 1. Open the .pdf/.svg in Illustrator and run "Foxy - 1 Prepare cut file" (moves magenta lines to the CUT layer).
 2. Add PageMARKs & BarCode on the CUT layer ("Foxy - 3 Batch barcodes" for the whole folder).
 3. Hide the CUT layer and print on 350gsm silk card (Versant), SRA4.
 4. Scan the barcode on the ColorCut, map Magenta = Cut.
 5. Test-cut the first sheet, then fit elastic + sticky tabs (Ready to Wear) or pack them loose (DIY).

 - Artwork is the owner's existing mask artwork from Dropbox ("2019 TIDY - CELEBRITY FACEMASKS FINAL 7200 IMAGES" and
