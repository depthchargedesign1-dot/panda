Personalised dog puffer jacket with an optional name

Adding a name is optional. Type your dog's name in the box above and we'll print it on the back in our North Yorkshire workshop, or leave it empty for a plain jacket. Pick your colour and size first. We print exactly what you enter, so please check names and spelling before you order. The print panel on a puffer is quite narrow, so shorter names fit best.

Why you'll love it

  • Padded, urban-style puffer: nylon shell and lining with a 200gsm polyester filling
  • Reinforced snap-poppers under the belly: no tricky zips
  • Reinforced slit so it goes on over your dog's harness
  • Adjustable back leg straps keep it in place on windy days
  • Name printed in-house on the back panel

Size & details

  • Back length (base of the neck to base of the tail): XS 25cm, S 30cm, M 35cm, L 40cm, XL 45cm, 2XL 50cm, 3XL 55cm, 4XL 60cm. If your dog is between sizes, go up one.
  • Colours: Black or Grey, every size.
  • Fabric: shell and lining 100% nylon; filling 100% polyester
  • Care: hand wash, maximum 40°C. Do not bleach, tumble dry, iron or dry clean.
