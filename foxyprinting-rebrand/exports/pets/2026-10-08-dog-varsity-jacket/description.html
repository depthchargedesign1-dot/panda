Our personalised dog varsity jacket gives your dog a proper college-sport look, with their name and number printed on the back if you'd like them. It's a fun gift for a birthday, a gotcha day or a new puppy or simply for looking smart at the park.

Personalised dog varsity jacket with optional name and number

Both personalisation boxes are optional. Add your dog's name, a number (up to 3 characters, such as a birthday, an age or a lucky number), both or neither, and we'll print exactly that on the back of the jacket in our North Yorkshire workshop. Leave both boxes empty and you'll get the plain jacket. Pick your colour and size, then check the live preview. We print exactly what you enter, so please check names and spelling before you order.

Why you'll love it

  • Sleek, satin-finish fabric with contrast striped ribbed sleeves and back hem for a classic varsity look
  • Lightweight and stretchy (94% polyester, 6% elastane, 280gsm), so they can run and play freely
  • Reinforced snap-poppers under the belly: no tricky zips, easy on and off
  • Reinforced slit so it goes on over your dog's harness
  • Adjustable back leg straps keep the coat in place on walks
  • Name and number printed in-house, so it's a dog coat nobody else will have

Size & details

  • Back length (base of the neck to base of the tail): XS 25cm, S 30cm, M 35cm, L 40cm, XL 45cm, 2XL 50cm, 3XL 55cm, 4XL 60cm. If your dog is between sizes, go up one.
  • Colours: Black, Navy or Pink with striped ribbed trims. 3XL and 4XL come in Black only.
  • Fabric: 94% polyester, 6% elastane; ribs 92% polyester, 8% elastane
  • Care: machine wash at 30°C on a mild cycle. Do not bleach, tumble dry, iron or dry clean.
