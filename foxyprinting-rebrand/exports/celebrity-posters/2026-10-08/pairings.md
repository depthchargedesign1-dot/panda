## 2. Frame-upgrade bundle – NOT made (doesn't fit how frames are sold)

How frames are sold today:
- Most of the other ~3,900 posters (e.g. the 758 NFL prints) have real Size variants: A4/A3/A2/A1 print only + A4/A3 Black or Silver frame (e.g. A. J. Green:
  £6.99 / £9.99 / £12.99 / £19.99 / framed A4 £19.99 / framed A3 £29.99).
- The other 9,475 "Printed Signature" posters are a single £6.99 variant; their sizes/frames come from an options app
  (they all sit in "All Posters – For size options"; retro posters use the Infinite Options "Poster Options" set).

A separate "frame upgrade" product would duplicate the framed variants, can't be tied to a particular poster in the
cart, and a frame-only price, frame material, depth and glazing are all **ASK** – so it would mislead buyers. Better
options for the owner to choose from: (a) a "Poster + Premium Display frame" line on every poster description (already
in the new collection copy); (b) an automatic discount, e.g. "Buy 2 framed posters, save 10%"; (c) if frames are to be
sold alone, tell us the frame-only prices per size and the frame material/depth.
